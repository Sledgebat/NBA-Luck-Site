How we check ★

What counts as right?

Our calls are about shooting, not minutes: "at his true shooting, with the same shots and minutes". So we check his scoring per shot: points divided by shots plus 0.44 for each free throw. A Defrost call is right if his scoring per shot went up after the call; a Heat check call if it went down.

When does a call count?

Once he's played five more games and taken 40 more shots. Before that it's too early to tell, and it isn't in the totals. Calls are saved the night they're made, as published; nothing is changed later.

Isn't a cool-off easy to call?

Easier than a bounce-back, yes: a hot streak is partly luck, and luck doesn't carry over. That's the point of the Heat check. The harder call is how far he'll fall, which is what "at his true shooting" puts a number on.
