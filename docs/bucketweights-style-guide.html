BucketWeights ★ Trash talkPg. 5

Trash talk

The questions we get most, answered straight.

So he's due?

No. Nobody is owed buckets. His next three goes in at his true level, about 38.5%, however cold he's been. The bounce-back is that rate catching up, not a make-up call.

Where does "true level" come from?

His last three seasons and this one, recent games counting more, pulled toward the league average until he's shot enough to trust. For threes that takes about 300 attempts; for free throws, about 25.

Can you see how open the shots were?

No. Public data doesn't track defenders. If a player's shots get harder, with a new team or a bigger role, we'll call it luck when it isn't. We say so on every page.

Do your calls come true?

About three in four of the big ones did over seven past seasons, and we show the misses. Zach LaVine stayed hot all of 2024-25.

Rules

  1. Loud cover, calm inside. The cover gets the issue colour, the logo, the overlap and one shadowed line. Inside pages are print: agate, card backs, letters, on page white or newsprint.
  2. Only stars and stripe rules. No tape, stamps, stickers, starbursts, grain, halftone overlays or tilted boxes.
  3. One drop shadow per screen, on the main coverline. Square corners everywhere.
  4. Hot and cold, not green and red, and colour is never the only signal: there's always a word or number.
  5. Every projection says what it holds fixed ("same shots and minutes") and shows a range or sample size.
  6. No logos or photos. Players are their names, set big. Teams are written out or abbreviated.
  7. No decorative motion.
  8. Phone first: one column at 375px, 16px gutters; the cover keeps the overlap at every width.
